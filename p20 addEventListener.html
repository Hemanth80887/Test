How to Handle Multiple Popups

  • Each popup opens independently and can be closed individually
  • Multiple popups can be open at the same time with proper stacking
  • Click the "X" button or outside the popup to close it
  • Use the "Close All Popups" button to close all open popups
  • The counter shows how many popups are currently active
  • Press ESC key to close the topmost popup
